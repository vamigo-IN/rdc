Questions

Dental care in Surat, answered

The things patients ask us most, before they book.

Is dental treatment at Roots Dental Care painless?
Yes. We use dental microscopes, digital X-rays and gentle technique, so most treatments, including root canals, are comfortable. Most nervous patients tell us it was nothing like they feared.
How much do dental implants and root canals cost in Surat?
We share the full cost with you before we begin, with no hidden charges, and offer no-cost EMI so larger treatments like implants and braces are easy to manage.
Where is Roots Dental Care located?
We are at T312, 4th Floor, Atlanta Shopping Center, Althan, Surat 395017, serving Althan, Vesu, Adajan and nearby areas of Surat.
What are your clinic timings?
We are open Monday to Saturday from 10 am to 8 pm, and Sunday from 10 am to 1:30 pm.
Do you offer same-day or emergency dental treatment?
Yes. Many treatments start the same day, and we see dental emergencies as quickly as we can. Message us on WhatsApp and we reply within minutes during clinic hours.
Do you treat children?
Yes. Our pediatric dentist gives gentle, child-friendly care, from check-ups and cleaning to fillings and preventive treatments.
