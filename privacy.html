Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

This policy explains what information Roots Dental Care collects when you contact us or book an appointment through this website, and how we use it. We keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Roots Dental Care is a multi-specialty dental clinic at T312, 4th Floor, Atlanta Shopping Center, Althan, Surat 395017. You can reach us on 79903 76179 or 79845 75670.

What we collect

  • Details you give us, your name, phone/WhatsApp number, the treatment you're interested in, and your preferred time, when you submit the booking form or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Basic usage data, if analytics is enabled, standard information such as pages viewed and device type, collected through cookies to measure how the site and our ads perform.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your appointment, by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To provide the dental care you ask about.
  • To understand which of our ads and pages are helpful, and improve them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services we use to run this website and respond to you:

  • WhatsApp (Meta), when your enquiry is delivered to our clinic chat.
  • Our email provider, to receive a copy of your booking request.
  • Google (Analytics & Ads) and Meta (Pixel), cookie-based tools that help us measure website and advertising performance. These may set cookies on your device.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and advertising measurement. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry details only as long as needed to help you and to meet our record-keeping obligations, then remove them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you've shared. Just contact us on the number above and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about your data? Call 79903 76179 or visit us at T312, 4th Floor, Atlanta Shopping Center, Althan, Surat 395017.
